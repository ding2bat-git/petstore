About Pet Haven SG

Founded in 2020, Pet Haven SG is Singapore's trusted online destination for premium pet supplies. We understand the unique needs of pets in our tropical climate and curate only the highest quality products from trusted brands worldwide.

Our mission is to make pet parenting joyful and convenient. With fast delivery across Singapore, excellent customer service, and a passion for animal welfare, we're here to support you and your furry, feathered, or scaled companions every step of the way.
